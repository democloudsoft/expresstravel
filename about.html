About Express Travel International

Dedicated travel consultation, airline ticketing, and holiday planning headquartered in Gulberg Green, Islamabad.

Company Profile

Your Local Travel Partner in Islamabad

Express Travel International is focused on simplifying travel arrangements for individuals, families, and corporate travelers departing from Islamabad and across Pakistan.

We understand that planning domestic or international journeys involves crucial decisions regarding routing, airline policies, visa documentation, and hotel accommodations. Our mission is to provide clear, honest, and practical travel solutions tailored to the real needs of our clients.

Conveniently located on Main Double Road in Gulberg Green, Islamabad, we welcome clients for in-person consultations as well as responsive phone and online assistance.

Our Philosophy

How We Approach Travel Service

We base our customer service around clarity, responsive communication, and dependable itinerary coordination.

Clear & Transparent Advice

We explain exact fare rules, baggage policies, refund restrictions, and required documentation before issuing tickets so you travel without unexpected surprises.

Personalized Attention

Every traveler has specific preferences—from direct routing and seat choices to family room configurations and budget requirements. We customize each plan accordingly.

Ongoing Support

Our relationship does not end when your e-ticket is issued. We assist with itinerary changes, date modifications, and travel updates whenever required.
