Privacy Policy

Information regarding the collection, handling, and security of passenger information and booking records.

1. Information We Collect

When you make an inquiry or book travel services with Express Travel International, we collect necessary personal details to process your reservations. This includes your full name (as recorded in your passport or CNIC), date of birth, passport details, contact phone numbers, email address, and itinerary preferences.

2. Use of Information

Your information is used strictly to communicate itinerary details, issue airline electronic tickets, confirm hotel accommodations, prepare visit visa documentation, and provide client service support.

3. Sharing with Third-Party Providers

To fulfill your bookings, relevant traveler details are transmitted securely to authorized airline reservation systems (GDS), hotel suppliers, visa processing portals, and transport operators. We do not sell or rent client data to independent marketing agencies.

4. Data Protection & Retention

We maintain reasonable physical and administrative safeguards to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

5. Contacting Us

If you have questions regarding our privacy practices or wish to review your booking details on file, please visit our office at Office No. 5, Near Adjacent Second Wife Restaurant & Cafe, Main Double Road, Gulberg Green, Islamabad, or call (051) 8777335.
