All flight fares, hotel rates, and tour package estimates are based on live inventory and exchange rates at the time of quotation. Quotations are not guaranteed until payment is received and tickets or vouchers are officially issued.

2. Traveler Responsibilities

Passengers are responsible for ensuring that all traveler names provided match their government-issued identity documents (CNIC / Passport) exactly. Travelers must verify that their passports hold at least six months validity from the date of return travel and that valid entry visas and health documentations are secured.

3. Cancellations & Date Changes

Ticket date change penalties, cancellation charges, and refund eligibility are dictated strictly by the operating airline, hotel supplier, or tour operator's tariff conditions. Express Travel International facilitates the processing of date change and refund requests according to these supplier rules.

4. Visa Consultation Limitation of Liability

Express Travel International provides visa guidance, document checklists, and reservation itineraries. Express Travel International has no role or authority in granting or rejecting visa applications. All decisions remain the sole jurisdiction of the respective foreign embassy or immigration department.
