OVERVIEW & ARCHITECTURE
------------------------------------------------------------------------
This website provides a complete, modern, responsive multi-page web 
experience for Express Travel International, an established travel 
agency based in Islamabad, Pakistan.

The codebase is built with clean HTML5, custom modern CSS3 styling 
(incorporating the charcoal/dark/black brand palette, Poppins font, 
and mobile responsiveness), and modular JavaScript for interactive 
multi-tab booking portals, accordion FAQs, dynamic modal dialogs, 
and direct WhatsApp connectivity.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
SITE MAP & PAGES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. index.html              - Homepage with Hero, Quick Booking Suite, Services, 
                             Featured Destinations, Why Choose Us, & FAQ.
2. about.html              - Company Profile, Mission, Vision, and Core Values.
3. services.html           - Detailed breakdown of all travel solutions.
4. flights.html            - Domestic Pakistan & International Air Ticketing with
                             custom flight inquiry request form.
5. hotels.html             - Worldwide hotel reservations and stay inquiries.
6. visas.html              - Visit visa assistance, requirements, document checklists,
                             and clear transparent policy disclaimers.
7. tours.html              - Domestic Pakistan (Hunza, Skardu) and International
                             (Dubai, Turkey, Maldives, Malaysia) tour packages.
8. destinations.html       - Destination showcase with flight & booking guidance.
9. blog.html               - Travel advice guides, baggage policies & visa tips.
10. faq.html               - Comprehensive Frequently Asked Questions accordion.
11. contact.html           - Islamabad office details, directions, and inquiry form.
12. booking.html           - Dedicated multi-service inquiry & booking portal.
13. privacy-policy.html    - Client data handling and protection policy.
14. terms-and-conditions.html - Ticketing, cancellation, and agency terms.
15. 404.html               - Clean fallback page.
