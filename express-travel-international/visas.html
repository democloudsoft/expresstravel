Transparent Visa Assistance Policy

Express Travel International acts as a travel consultant assisting applicants with document organization, form completion, flight itinerary reservations, and hotel vouchers. We do not issue visas, and we do not guarantee visa issuance. Final approval or refusal decisions are made exclusively at the discretion of the respective embassy, consulate, or immigration authority.
